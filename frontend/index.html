How CampusFix Works

A streamlined, transparent 5-step process connecting students directly with administrators and field technicians.

1
Report

Submit issue details with location details & drag-and-drop photos.

2
Verify

Administration reviews complaint urgency and prioritizes requests.

3
Assign

Task assigned to specialized departmental technical staff.

4
Resolve

Technician performs repair and uploads completion proof image.

5
Track

Real-time status updates reflecting on student dashboard.

Key Features

Designed to deliver complete transparency and accountability for institutional operations.

Easy Complaint Reporting

Intuitive reporting form allowing students to log issues under specific categories in seconds.

GPS Location Support

Capture precise geolocation coordinates to help technicians locate exact classroom or building spots.

Photo Evidence Upload

Drag-and-drop file upload for attaching photos of damaged equipment or electrical faults.

Real-Time Status Tracking

Audit trail timeline detailing status transitions from Pending to In Progress and Resolved.

Departmental Staff Assignment

Administrators dispatch complaints directly to assigned technicians in Electrical, IT, or Plumbing.

Admin Analytics Dashboard

High-level overview of total, pending, in-progress, and resolved campus maintenance metrics.
